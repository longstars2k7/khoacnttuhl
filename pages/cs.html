Computer Science Program at Ha Long University

In the context of the Fourth Industrial Revolution, Computer Science has affirmed its important role in almost all fields, from industry and agriculture to education and healthcare. In Vietnam, this field is rapidly growing, especially in major cities such as Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and Da Nang. According to TopDev’s report on the Vietnam IT market, the demand for IT human resources increases every year, with a shortage of approximately 150,000 to 195,000 programmers and engineers.

This shortage not only poses significant challenges but also offers great opportunities for those pursuing Computer Science. The strong wave of foreign investment in technology companies in Vietnam has created thousands of jobs with competitive salaries. The skills and knowledge gained from Computer Science not only help students meet domestic demands but also open up international career opportunities.

At Ha Long University, the Computer Science curriculum is designed to provide a solid knowledge foundation, helping students confidently meet the increasing demands of the job market. The program includes:

Fundamental Knowledge

Students will study core subjects such as:

Introduction to Computer Science: Overview of the field, computer structure, and basic programming concepts.
Data Structures and Algorithms: Skills for designing and optimizing algorithms for data processing.
Operating Systems: Understanding how operating systems manage resources and support users.
Computer Networks: Learning about network protocols, security, and data communication models.

After the foundational stage, students will explore specialized areas such as:

Software Development: Applying modern methodologies such as Agile and Scrum in software development.
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: Studying AI algorithms, neural networks, and real-world applications.
Data Science and Big Data Analytics: Using modern tools and technologies to analyze large-scale data.
Cybersecurity: Protecting systems from increasingly complex threats.

Practical Training and Real-world Applications

In addition to theory, students practice with the latest technologies through modern laboratories. Ha Long University also organizes seminars, group projects, and internship programs with leading technology companies.

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the Computer Science program can take on important roles such as:

Software Engineer / Developer: Develop mobile applications, websites, and software systems.
Data Scientist: Collect, analyze, and make predictions from data.
System and Database Administrator: Design, manage, and maintain complex data systems.
Lecturer or Researcher: Teach and conduct research at universities or research institutes.
Salaries in this field typically range from 10 to 30 million VND/month for fresh graduates and can be higher depending on position and experience.

Admission Subject Combinations

The Computer Science program considers the following subject combinations:

A00: Math, Physics, Chemistry
A01: Math, Physics, English
C01: Literature, Math, Physics
C02: Literature, Math, Chemistry
D01: Literature, Math, English
D07: Math, Chemistry, English
X02: Math, Literature, Informatics
X06: Math, Physics, Informatics

Required Qualities for Success

Computer Science requires not only passion but also the following qualities:

Logical and creative thinking: Essential for solving complex problems.
Teamwork and communication skills: Help you collaborate effectively in projects.
Patience and attention to detail: Important when working with complex code.
Lifelong learning mindset: Technology evolves constantly, so you must always be ready to learn.

The Computer Science program at Ha Long University not only offers attractive career opportunities but also serves as a gateway to exploring and mastering the world of technology. With a modern curriculum, advanced facilities, and dedicated lecturers, it is an ideal place to start your journey in the IT field.
