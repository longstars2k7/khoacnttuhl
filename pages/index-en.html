Faculty of Information Technology

Vice Dean in Charge: PhD Candidate LÊ MẠNH HÀ

The Faculty of Information Technology offers undergraduate programs in Computer Science, Information Technology, Graphic Design, and Informatics Education, as well as a Master’s program in Computer Science.

Many students have achieved awards in scientific research competitions at various levels. Each year, the Faculty collaborates with the Quang Ninh Youth Union and the Department of Education and Training to successfully organize the Quang Ninh Young Informatics Competition. It also partners with Hanoi University of Science and Technology to host the Thinking Skills Assessment (TSA).

The Faculty was awarded the title of “Excellent Labor Collective” in 2022, and many staff members have received certificates of merit from the Ministry of Education and Training, the Quang Ninh Provincial People’s Committee, and youth organizations at both provincial and national levels.
